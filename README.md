# Global-Super-Store-Data-Analysis
Global Super Store is an Online Shopping Store which was most popular during COVID time as Customers bought anything they want conveniently.Customer and Product Analysis are conducted on the Global Super Store dataset using Excel, Power Query, Pivot table, Pivot chart, Slicers and Conditional Formatting.KPI results are displayed in Excel Dashboard.

## Project Overview
This project explores the Global Superstore dataset to analyze sales, profit, customers, products, countries, and delivery performance. Using Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers, I transformed and analyzed the data to identify key business trends and performance drivers. The findings were presented through an interactive Excel dashboard with KPIs and key business insights.

## Dataset
The dataset used in this project is the Global Super Store Dataset, sourced from Kaggle. It contains transactional sales data covering orders, customers, products, sales, profit, discounts, shipping, and geographic information across multiple countries and regions.

The dataset was used to analyze:
Sales and profit performance
Customer segments and customer behavior
Product and category performance
Country and regional performance
Discounts and profitability
Delivery time and shipping performance

Source : https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/apoorvaappz/global-super-store-dataset

## Data Preparation
Cleaning and Transformation Steps Performed :
- The Row number and Quantity changed to data type Whole Number. Order Id, Ship Mode, Customer ID, Customer Name, Segment, City, State, Country, Postal Code, Market, Region, Product ID, Category, Sub-Category, Product Name, Order Priority Data types changed from ABC 123 (Any) to Text.
- Order Date and Ship Date changed to date format DD-MM-YYYY.
- Sales, Discount, Profit, Shipping Cost columns are chosen the decimal Data type.
- To check missing values and blank cells, in the power query there is filter icon for each column to select if there are any null values or blank cells and can be checked.
- Here in the data, Postal Code column has around 80% null values. This field is chosen to be text data type and null values are ignored as it is not much of use for our analysis.
- The text data type columns alone have to be trimmed to get rid of extra or trailing spaces before and after the text values. Select the columns and choose Transform -> Format -> Trim and again Transform -> Format -> Clean to get a clean data without any extra spaces.
- Checked Data Consistency so as when the data is viewed in Pivot table or Pivot Chart the category in rows does not split into multiple rows and give wrong results.
- New calculated columns Delivery Time in Days and Unit Price were added to the Original Dataset to perform Product analysis.

## Key Business Insights
- The Consumer segment is highly profitable followed by Corporate and Home Office segments for the Global Super Store Company consecutively from the year 2011 - 2014.
- The United States is the largest revenue-generating market, while India, China, and the United Kingdom deliver higher profit margins. Indonesia has relatively strong sales but the lowest margin among the Top 10.
- The combined profit generated by the Top 5 products increased substantially from 2011 to 2014. This indicates that the contribution from the highest-profit products strengthened over the period.
- The Profit Margin calculation indicated that the High Revenue Customers are more profitable than High Frequent buying Customers.
- The Product sales is inversely proportional to Product price that is the Product sales increases with decrease in Product price at day level.
- The Highest number of Customers are from USA while the lowest are from Bahrain,Burundi,Chad,Eritrea,South Sudan and Swaziland.
- The Average Delivery Time across Countries is 3.97 days. The countries above the benchmark may have opportunities for delivery- efficiency  improvements.
